Our Story

About Bromantic Enterprise

We turn ideas into real, high-quality 3D printed objects — from fun keychains to detailed display pieces and custom props.

Who We Are

Bromantic Enterprise is a 3D printing service based in Selangor, Malaysia. We specialize in creating custom and ready-made 3D printed products for everyday use, gifting, display, and cosplay. Whether you need a single unique piece or a small batch, we focus on quality, detail, and reliable delivery.

What We Offer

  • • Keychains & small accessories
  • • Toys and articulated figures
  • • Display models and miniatures
  • • Cosplay props and accessories
  • • Fully custom designs based on your ideas or files
