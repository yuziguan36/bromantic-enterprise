Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does 3D printing take? ▼

Most small items (keychains, small toys) can be ready within 1–3 days. Larger or more complex pieces may take 5–10 days depending on size, quantity and finishing. We’ll confirm the timeline when we give you a quote.

Can I choose the color? ▼

Yes. We offer a range of filament colors. You can specify your preferred color when placing an order or requesting a custom quote.

Can I provide my own 3D model / design? ▼

Absolutely. You can send us STL, OBJ or similar files. If you only have images or sketches, we can discuss whether design work is needed (may involve additional cost).

Is there a minimum order quantity? ▼

No strict minimum. We accept single pieces for custom work. For repeated designs, larger quantities usually have better unit pricing.

How do I get a quote? ▼

The easiest way is to message us on WhatsApp or submit a request through the Custom Order page. Include a description, size, color preference and any reference images. We’ll reply with price and estimated time.

Do you ship across Malaysia? ▼

Yes, we can arrange delivery within Malaysia. Shipping cost depends on location and package size. Self-collection in Selangor is also available.
